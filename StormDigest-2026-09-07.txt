--- 1. TROPICAL STORM EDOUARD AFTERMATH (Sept 2-5) ---
Damage concentrated in non-qualifying east Texas counties.

Edouard (landfall Sept 1 near Port Arthur/Johnson Bayou area) moved inland and
stalled, producing catastrophic rainfall in east Texas:
- 20+ inches recorded in Hardin and Tyler counties
- Flash Flood Emergency issued for Hardin and Tyler counties
- 21 people rescued from assisted living facility in Hardin County
- Polk County issued local disaster declaration (Sept 2)
- Flood warnings active through Sept 4

Counties with Edouard damage: Jefferson, Hardin, Tyler, Orange, Polk.
NONE are qualifying coastal counties. No federal DR- issued for Edouard as of
Sept 7 search date.

Note: Jefferson County (Port Arthur area) is not on the qualifying coastal counties
list despite its coastal location. It is not among the 10 qualifying counties
(Aransas, Nueces, San Patricio, Refugio, Calhoun, Victoria, Jackson, Matagorda,
Brazoria, Galveston).

The Sept 2 digest already captured Edouard's qualifying impact on Galveston County,
Brazoria County, and Matagorda County (tropical storm warnings, storm surge).
This window's Edouard findings are aftermath only — interior flooding, no new
coastal-county qualifying event.

--- 2. JULY 2026 FLOODING — FEMA MAJOR DISASTER DECLARATION (Sept 3) ---
Federal DR- approved for 19 interior counties. No qualifying coastal counties.

Governor Abbott announced on Sept 3 that FEMA approved a Major Disaster Declaration
for Texas summer severe weather and flooding (July 2026 event). Public Assistance
approved for:
  Bandera, Crockett, Dimmit, Edwards, Frio, Gillespie, Kendall, Kerr, Kimble,
  Kinney, La Salle, Mason, Maverick, Menard, Real, Sutton, Uvalde, Val Verde, Zavala

All 19 counties are interior Texas. None are qualifying coastal counties.
Individual Assistance request for 30 counties remains pending as of Sept 7.

DOES NOT QUALIFY.

--- 3. BEXAR COUNTY (SAN ANTONIO) — DAMAGE ASSESSMENT IN PROGRESS ---
Preliminary damage assessments underway. No federal declaration issued yet.

Governor Abbott directed SBA, state, and local officials to conduct Joint Preliminary
Damage Assessments (JPDAs) for Bexar County following the August 28-29 severe
windstorm (previously covered in Aug 31 and Sept 2 digests). A JPDA is a federal
disaster declaration eligibility step — it is NOT itself a declaration.

Status: Under evaluation. No federal DR- issued for Bexar County as of Sept 7.

Note: The August 28-29 San Antonio storm already qualifies under the "widespread
structural damage" criterion (75 structural inspections, 2 commercial buildings
demolished, ~$10M damage estimate — all KSAT-sourced) and was captured in the
Aug 31 and Sept 2 digests. A Bexar County federal declaration, if issued, would
add a FEMA qualifier but is not needed for the prior qualification.
