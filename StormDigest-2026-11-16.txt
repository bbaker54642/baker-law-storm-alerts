FACEBOOK POST — EVENT 1:
---
FEMA opened disaster recovery centers for the July-August 2026 Texas storms on November 11. The deadline to apply for individual assistance is December 29, 2026. [1]

Twenty Texas counties are eligible: Bandera, Bexar, Comal, Crockett, Dimmit, Frio, Gillespie, Kendall, Kerr, Kimble, Kinney, La Salle, Maverick, Medina, Real, Schleicher, Sutton, Uvalde, Val Verde, and Zavala. [1]

Here is what people miss: having homeowners or renters insurance does not disqualify you from FEMA individual assistance. FEMA IA covers unmet needs that insurance does not pay. It is a grant, not a loan. [2]

Recovery centers are open in San Antonio at Las Palmas Branch Library, in Kerrville at Calvary Temple Church, and also in Sonora (Sutton County) and Ozona (Crockett County). Apply at DisasterAssistance.gov or by calling 800-621-3362. [1]

The Texas Workforce Commission disaster unemployment assistance deadline for these storms is also December 29, 2026. [3]

Your homeowners insurance policy has a separate deadline for filing a claim. That deadline comes from your policy, not from FEMA. FEMA's December 29 deadline does not extend your insurance deadline, and your insurance deadline does not extend FEMA's.

FACEBOOK POST — EVENT 2:
---
A line of severe thunderstorms crossed the Texas coast on November 15. The National Weather Service in Houston issued a severe thunderstorm warning for Matagorda County with estimated wind gusts of 60 miles per hour. Bay City was directly in the warning area. Damage estimates for the region are still being assessed. [1]

This came alongside coastal flood advisories covering Aransas, Nueces, Calhoun, Galveston, and Brazoria counties through November 16. [2]

After storms like this, I typically see the coverage dispute start before the cleanup is done. TWIA covers direct wind damage. NFIP policies cover rising water. The question is what the adjuster characterizes when they see water inside a structure. If they describe it as rain intrusion through a wall or roof that was intact before the storm, TWIA reduces the wind loss. If they describe the opening as a pre-existing condition, they reduce it further.

The same water damage in the same structure can be characterized two different ways depending on who is measuring. The first written inspection report sets the frame for everything that follows.

Your NFIP policy requires a proof of loss within 60 days of the date of loss. For this event, that clock started November 15. The deadline falls around January 14, 2027.
