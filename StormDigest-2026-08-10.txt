   As of August 10, 2026, the National Hurricane Center is monitoring
   3 areas for possible tropical development in the Atlantic basin.
   The most significant is a broad area of low pressure in the eastern
   and central tropical Atlantic with a 60% formation probability over

2. NOAA MAINTAINS BELOW-NORMAL SEASON FORECAST
   NOAA released its August update to the 2026 hurricane outlook,

   below-normal due to El Nino conditions first emerging in the
   equatorial Pacific in June. Two named storms have occurred so far
   in 2026 (Arthur and Bertha); neither produced qualifying damage
