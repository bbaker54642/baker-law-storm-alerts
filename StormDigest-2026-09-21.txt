STORM: TWIA 0% Rate Filing — TDI Public Hearing September 23, 2026
---
On Tuesday, September 23, the Texas Department of Insurance holds a public hearing on
TWIA's rate filing for 2027 policies.[1] This year TWIA filed for a 0% rate change.
The board voted 7 to 1 to hold rates steady.[2] TDI has until October 15 to approve or
reject the filing.[1]

A 0% filing sounds like good news for policyholders. It may be.

What matters for a TWIA policyholder is not just the premium amount but what the policy
covers and how TWIA handles claims. Rates and coverage are two separate things. Rates can
stay flat while claim practices change.

TWIA's own analysis finds current rates adequate by 9% for residential policies and 4%
for commercial.[2] That is the first time in years the association has found its rates
above the line. The shift comes from legislative changes, not a reduction in storm risk.

People who own property in TWIA-eligible coastal counties pay TWIA premiums as a matter
of necessity. Private market windstorm capacity on the Texas coast is thin. For most
coastal property owners, TWIA is the only available windstorm insurer.

The TDI hearing is where the public and industry weigh in. Written comments are due to
TDI by October 1.[1] The Commissioner's decision deadline is October 15.[1] After that
date, the 0% filing either stands or is modified.
