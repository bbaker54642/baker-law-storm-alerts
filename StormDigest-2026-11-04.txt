QUALIFYING EVENT 1 — SBA PHYSICAL DAMAGE LOANS OPEN FOR FEMA-4935-DR IA COUNTIES (NEW — TODAY)
========================================================
Event: SBA disaster physical damage loan window opened today for 20 FEMA-4935-DR Individual Assistance counties; deadline December 30, 2026.
Criterion met: (b) FEMA Individual Assistance counties; (d) widespread structural damage confirmed from July 2026 storms (2,944 structures damaged, 132 destroyed, 618 major damage per state's FEMA appeal).

Summary:
Federal Register notice 2026-22469, published November 4, 2026, formalizes the SBA disaster loan program under FEMA-4935-DR following the October 30, 2026 IA designation. SBA physical damage loans are now open for homeowners, renters, businesses, and nonprofits in all 20 IA-designated counties.

Key dates:
- Physical damage loan application deadline: December 30, 2026
- Economic injury (EIDL) deadline: July 30, 2027

Loan limits (confirmed in Federal Register notice):
- Homeowners: up to $500,000 for real estate (structural/property damage)
- Homeowners and renters: up to $100,000 for personal property
- Businesses and nonprofits: up to $2 million

SBA loan numbers: Physical damage #220446; Economic injury #220450

How to apply: lending.sba.gov or 800-659-2955

20 counties covered: Bandera, Bexar, Comal, Crockett, Dimmit, Frio, Gillespie, Kendall, Kerr, Kimble, Kinney, La Salle, Maverick, Medina, Real, Schleicher, Sutton, Uvalde, Val Verde, Zavala.

NOTE ON SBA BEXAR COUNTY SEPARATE DEADLINE: The August 28, 2026 Bexar County storms are covered by a separate SBA administrative declaration (Federal Register 2026-18777) with a physical damage loan deadline of November 9, 2026 — 5 days from today. This is a different program for a different event.

FACEBOOK POST — EVENT 1:
---
The SBA physical damage loan window for the 20 Texas counties affected by the July 2026 storms is now open. The Federal Register published the SBA program notice today. Physical damage loan applications are due December 30, 2026. [1]

The 20 counties are: Bandera, Bexar, Comal, Crockett, Dimmit, Frio, Gillespie, Kendall, Kerr, Kimble, Kinney, La Salle, Maverick, Medina, Real, Schleicher, Sutton, Uvalde, Val Verde, and Zavala. These are the same counties designated for FEMA Individual Assistance on October 30. [1]

Loan limits: homeowners up to $500,000 for real estate losses; businesses and nonprofits up to $2 million; homeowners and renters up to $100,000 for personal property. [1]

Apply at lending.sba.gov or call 800-659-2955. [2]

This is a separate program from FEMA Individual Assistance. It covers the same categories of loss at higher amounts, but the funds are loans, not grants. [2]

An SBA disaster loan and a private insurance claim are not mutually exclusive. Applying for an SBA loan does not close your insurance claim or waive any right to dispute a denial or underpayment. [2]

If you are in Bexar County and your property was damaged in the August 28, 2026 storms: a different SBA loan program covers that event and the physical damage application deadline is November 9, 2026 — this Sunday. [3]

[1] Federal Register 2026-22469, Nov. 4, 2026. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/04/2026-22469/presidential-declaration-of-a-major-disaster-for-the-state-of-texas
[2] SBA disaster loan assistance. https://www.sba.gov/disaster
[3] Federal Register 2026-18777. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/14/2026-18777/administrative-declaration-of-a-disaster-for-the-state-of-texas
