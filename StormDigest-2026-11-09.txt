FACEBOOK POST — EVENT 1:
---
South Texas is flooding. Flash flood warnings covered Kleberg, Duval, Jim Wells, and Nueces counties beginning November 8. Oso Creek in Corpus Christi is forecast to crest at 23 feet tonight, 3 feet above flood stage. Aransas County, including Rockport, is under a flood watch and a coastal flood advisory through tomorrow morning. Governor Abbott deployed Texas A&M Task Force 1 and Task Force 3 for swiftwater rescue in the Coastal Bend. [1][2]

For property owners whose structures took on water today: document everything before you clean it up. Photograph every room. Mark the water line on walls before it dries. Record the date and time. These records matter when an adjuster determines cause of loss weeks later. [3]

On the Texas coast, water in a structure raises a coverage question that determines which policy responds. TWIA covers wind damage. The National Flood Insurance Program covers rising floodwater. Storm surge is treated differently under each policy. Adjusters for each carrier characterize damage to minimize what their policy owes. The documentation you create today is the evidence to counter that characterization. [3]

If your structure flooded from this event, your NFIP proof-of-loss deadline runs 60 days from the date of loss. That clock does not pause for the adjustment process. [3]
