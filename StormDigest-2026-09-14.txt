DRAFT POST — TWIA SECOND-HOMES COVERAGE VOTE
==============================================================================

STORM/DEVELOPMENT: TWIA Coverage Vote — Galveston County Second Homes

---
On September 1, the TWIA board committee voted 2 to 1 to drop a proposal that
would have eliminated windstorm coverage for 53,620 second homes and beach
properties across the Texas coast.[1] Only 4 of 295 written public comments
supported the proposal.[1]

Galveston County alone has 18,178 TWIA policies on second homes and beach
cabins.[1] Under the proposal, every one of those policies would have ended.

I have watched TWIA's policy decisions for years. This one matters. TWIA is
the windstorm insurer of last resort for coastal Texas. When private carriers
won't write windstorm coverage, TWIA is what is available. A beach house that
loses TWIA coverage does not automatically find a replacement. Private market
capacity on the Texas coast for windstorm is thin.

The committee voted no this time. That vote was 2 to 1. The proposal can come
back. The board meets regularly, and coverage decisions can change with the
agenda.

If you own a beach property or vacation home in a TWIA-eligible county, your
windstorm coverage is not permanent by default. It is subject to board decisions.
The public comment process is real, and those comments carried weight this time.[1]

Storm damage is one thing people with coastal properties prepare for. Coverage
loss is another.

SOURCES:
[1] https://kfdm.com/news/local/twia-makes-key-decision-on-coverage-for-53620-second-homes-beach-cabins
[2] https://www.twia.org/public-invited-to-comment-as-twia-committee-continues-work-on-report-to-legislature-at-september-1-meeting/
