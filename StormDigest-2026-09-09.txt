MONITORED DEVELOPMENTS (Non-Qualifying)

--- 1. EDOUARD FEMA DECLARATION — NO FEDERAL DR ISSUED ---
As of September 9, no FEMA Major Disaster Declaration has been approved for any
county affected by Tropical Storm Edouard (landfall Sept 1). Local declarations
were issued by Jefferson County, Port Arthur, Hardin County, Polk County, Orange
County, and Chambers County. FEMA conducted a debris assessment for Hardin County
and determined the amount and extent of debris does NOT meet FEMA criteria for
federally-funded debris removal. No federal DR appears imminent for Edouard.
Source: KFDM — feds-not-enough-storm-related-debris-in-hardin-county-for-federally-funded-removal

Note: Qualifying coastal counties (Galveston, Brazoria, Matagorda) saw minor impacts
from Edouard — already covered in the Sept 2 digest. A federal declaration for
those counties would add a FEMA qualifier to that already-documented event, but
the evidence trail now suggests it is unlikely.

--- 2. BEXAR COUNTY (SAN ANTONIO) — STILL IN ASSESSMENT PHASE ---
No federal FEMA disaster declaration has been approved for Bexar County from the
August 28-29 windstorm. Governor Abbott directed Joint Preliminary Damage Assessments
(SBA + state + local) for Bexar County — this is a precursor step, not a declaration.
Residents are self-reporting losses via TDEM's iSTAT tool. Process is ongoing with
no approval or denial as of September 9.
Sources: Houston Public Media, TPR (Texas Public Radio), CBS Austin

The August 28-29 San Antonio storm already qualifies in prior digests (Aug 31 and
Sept 2) under widespread structural damage and sourced $10M damage estimate. A federal
declaration, if eventually issued, would add a FEMA qualifier but is not needed for
the prior qualification.

--- 3. JULY 2026 FEMA DR-4935 — UNCHANGED ---
FEMA-4935-DR (signed September 1, published Federal Register September 9) covers
the July 2026 severe storms and flooding. Public Assistance approved for 19 counties
(Bandera, Crockett, Dimmit, Edwards, Frio, Gillespie, Kendall, Kerr, Kimble,
Kinney, La Salle, Mason, Maverick, Menard, Real, Sutton, Uvalde, Val Verde, Zavala).
All interior Texas. Governor's IA request for 30 counties remains pending; the
specific counties were not confirmed in available sources.
Source: federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/09/2026-18354/...
