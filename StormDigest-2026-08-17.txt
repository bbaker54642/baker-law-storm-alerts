4. CRYSTAL CITY / ZAVALA COUNTY — FEMA INDIVIDUAL ASSISTANCE GAP
   As of August 14, KERA News (NPR Dallas) reported that residents
   of Crystal City (Zavala County) are still rebuilding with limited
   federal help. The Zavala County Sheriff's Office documented 180
   structures with flood damage ranging from minimal to severe.
   Despite Zavala County's inclusion in the FEMA emergency declaration
   (EM-3649-TX, July 18-19), local leaders expressed frustration
   that a separate Major Disaster Declaration — which would activate
   FEMA Individual Assistance (IA) grants directly to affected
   homeowners — had not yet been approved as of August 14.
   NOTE: This distinction matters for Kevin's practice: emergency
   declarations provide Public Assistance (government reimbursement)
   but not IA (direct homeowner grants). Residents who believe they
   are covered by the declaration may be surprised to find they
   cannot apply for FEMA IA grants without a separate DR approval.
   Sources: KERA News Aug 14 (keranews.org/news/2026-08-14/crystal-city-texas-flood-recovery-fema-federal-aid);
            TPR Aug 15 (tpr.org/environment/2026-08-15/in-this-flood-ravaged-south-texas-town-residents-are-rebuilding-while-waiting-for-federal-aid);
            KENS5 (kens5.com — mandatory evacuations Crystal City);
            FEMA EM-3649-TX (fema.gov/disaster/3649)
